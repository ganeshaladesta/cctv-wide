## 1. National Overview & Discovery Summary

Through comprehensive reverse engineering of official municipal ATCS portals, provincial Smart City platforms, national toll-road/transportation aggregators, and public government endpoints, we have cataloged **23,574 Indonesian public CCTV cameras** (excluding 898 non-Indonesian feeds present in the reference dataset) across **all 38 provinces of Indonesia** and **610+ distinct government/institutional sources**.

### 1.1 Major Source Categories
1. **Municipal & Regency Dishub ATCS / Diskominfo Smart City Portals (~350+ sources)**:
   - Operated by Dinas Perhubungan (Dishub) and Dinas Komunikasi dan Informatika (Diskominfo) at the Kota and Kabupaten levels.
   - Common media servers: **MediaMTX / go2rtc**, **Datarhei Restreamer** (`/memfs/<uuid>.m3u8`), **Shinobi** (`/<key>/hls/<group>/<id>/s.m3u8`), **Ant Media Server** (`/LiveApp/streams/<id>.m3u8` or `/WebRTCApp/streams/<id>.m3u8`), **ZoneMinder** (`/zm/cgi-bin/nph-zms`), and **Wowza Streaming Engine** (`playlist.m3u8`).
2. **National Highway & Toll Road Systems (`Bina Marga`, `Jasa Marga`, `Hutama Karya`, `BUJT`, ~11,800+ cameras)**:
   - Operated by Kementerian PU (Ditjen Bina Marga), PT Jasa Marga (`jmlive.jasamarga.com`), PT Hutama Karya (`pub.hk-opt.com`, `pub2.hk-opt.com` for Trans-Sumatera), PT Jakarta Tollroad Development (`camera.jtd.co.id`), PT Waskita Bumi Wira (`cctv.waskitabumiwira.com`), PT Semesta Marga Raya (`cctv.smr.web.id`), and Kemenhub Mudik / Terminal portals (`mudik.kemenhub.go.id/cctv`).
3. **Provincial Aggregators**:
   - **DI Yogyakarta**: `cctv.jogjaprov.go.id` (620 cameras across Sleman, Bantul, Kota Jogja, Kulon Progo, Gunungkidul).
   - **DKI Jakarta**: Jakarta Smart City / BaliTower (`dki-jkt.balitower.co.id`, `serve02.molecool.id`, 1,240+ cameras).
   - **Banten**: Dishub Provinsi Banten (`cctv.bantenprov.go.id`, 44 ATCS cameras).
   - **Bali**: Bali Satu Data (`transcode.baliprov.go.id`, 91 cameras).
4. **National Public Institutional Network (Mahkamah Agung / Ditjen Badilag ACO — 440+ Satker across all 38 Provinces)**:
   - Operated via `cctv.badilag.net` (`*.cctvbadilag.my.id`, `*.cctvbadilag2.my.id`, `*.cctvbadilag3.my.id`, `cctvstream.badilag.net`) using Ant Media Server with `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *`, providing verified live public outdoor/courtyard/parking/PTSP streams in remote regencies across Maluku, Papua, Sulawesi, Kalimantan, and Nusa Tenggara where municipal Dishub ATCS portals do not yet exist.
